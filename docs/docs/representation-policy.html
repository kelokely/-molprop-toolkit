Structure of record

Representation policy is part of the method, not bookkeeping

Salts, tautomers, stereochemistry, and protomer choices can change similarity, clustering, 3D descriptors, and human interpretation. MolScope makes those choices explicit so teams can compare runs without pretending the representation step never happened.

Salt and solvate stripping

A supplied salt form may be the right experimental record, but the stripped parent is usually the right structure for similarity, clustering, and most descriptor comparisons. MolScope keeps both stories visible.

Tautomer handling

Two tautomeric forms can move hydrogen-bonding and aromaticity features enough to change risk flags or nearest-neighbor relationships. Use prep audit mode when tautomer choice is part of the chemistry question.

Stereochemistry

Undefined stereocenters are not a neutral detail. They change uniqueness, depictions, and any interpretation that depends on 3D or conformer-derived fields.

Protomers near assay pH

Heuristic ionization and protomer handling help normalize ranking, but they are still coarse policy choices rather than rigorous microstate modeling.

When to use each prep preset

triage

Use for fast project-wide ranking when you want one pragmatic parent form and an auditable but lightweight structure policy.

audit

Use when ambiguity itself is important and the team wants to inspect how salts, tautomers, stereochemistry, or protonation may change downstream conclusions.

3d

Use when conformer generation or 3D-sensitive outputs will be used downstream and the working protomer/stereo choice needs to be explicit.

external

Use when the project has already made a representation choice upstream and MolScope should preserve rather than reinterpret that decision.
