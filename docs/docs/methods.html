Method cards for the main science blocks

MolScope combines deterministic structure handling, descriptor calculations, rule-style heuristics, measured-data joins, and campaign-derived summaries. These cards explain what each layer does and where its use boundary should stop.

Stable Representation policy Deterministic transforms

Structure preparation and traceability

What it computes Standardized parent representations, tautomer/protomer handling, stereo audit fields, and structure-of-record columns such as Input_Canonical_SMILES, Canonical_SMILES, Calc_Base_SMILES, and Calc_Canonical_SMILES.
Scientific basis Reproducible structure standardization and explicit representation tracking so downstream similarity, clustering, and descriptors operate on an understood molecular form.
Known limitations Tautomer or protomer selection is still a policy choice. It does not guarantee biological relevance or capture the full solution ensemble.
Recommended use boundary Use it to make representation decisions explicit and comparable across runs, especially before 3D or series workflows.
Stable Calculated descriptors Rule-style filters

Descriptors, CNS MPO, and drug-likeness frameworks

What it computes 2D descriptors, CNS MPO, oral and fragment-style filters, QED, and lead metrics used for ranking and triage.
Scientific basis Established descriptor families plus literature-inspired desirability and rule frameworks such as CNS MPO, Rule of 5, Rule of 3, and related med-chem filter sets.
Known limitations These are balancing signals, not direct predictors of potency, efficacy, or human PK.
Recommended use boundary Compare analog families, identify drift, and communicate property balance. Do not read a single score as a decision on its own.
Stable Liability heuristics Descriptor context

Alerts, metabolism, hERG, CYP, and med-chem flags

What it computes Rule-style alerts, motif flags, burden summaries, and qualitative classes for known liability families.
Scientific basis Recurring medicinal chemistry liability patterns, motif catalogs, and property contexts that often correlate with later experimental trouble.
Known limitations Alert burden is not a calibrated probability of failure. False positives and project-specific exceptions are expected.
Recommended use boundary Use these signals to choose counterscreens, orthogonal assays, and targeted de-risking chemistry rather than as hard gates.
Stable Exposure heuristics Composite scores

Solubility, permeability, PK, and developability composites

What it computes Solubility and permeability heuristics, PK-facing classifications, and Dev_* developability burden outputs.
Scientific basis Descriptor combinations and pragmatic composite logic informed by familiar med-chem filters such as Pfizer 3/75, GSK 4/400, and golden-triangle-style reasoning.
Known limitations These outputs are coarse proxies for experiment planning. They are not replacements for measured ADME, PK, or formulation work.
Recommended use boundary Use them to prioritize what to test next and to explain why a series may need a broader redesign rather than small potency-first tweaks.
Preview Measured data Campaign-derived summaries

Assay integration, compare, SAR, MMP, learnings, and dashboard

What it computes Measured-data joins, round-over-round deltas, scaffold-centric summaries, matched-pair transforms, reusable learnings, and persistent campaign dashboards.
Scientific basis Straightforward table joins, explicit provenance tracking, scaffold grouping, transform extraction, and campaign-derived aggregation rather than black-box prediction.
Known limitations These outputs are only as reliable as identifier joins, series context, and the amount of measured evidence available in the table.
Recommended use boundary Use them to compare rounds, preserve project memory, and summarize what the campaign learned. Treat sparse or heuristic-only lessons as tentative.
Preview Optional dependency Route prioritization

Retrosynthesis reranking

What it computes Reranked route summaries and retro-enriched tables using AiZynthFinder outputs plus MolScope policies such as balanced, stock-first, score-first, and shortest.
Scientific basis Optional AiZynthFinder route generation followed by transparent post-processing over route score, precursor stock coverage, and step count.
Known limitations Route scores and stock availability are planning aids, not guarantees of synthetic feasibility, cost, or execution speed.
Recommended use boundary Use reranking as one factor in “make next” prioritization, especially when several compounds look otherwise similar on property balance.
