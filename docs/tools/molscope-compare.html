Campaign compare

Compare two MolScope-compatible tables and summarize overlap, endpoint shifts, and group-level changes so a team can ask round-over-round questions without rebuilding spreadsheets by hand.

Command: molscope compare.

Typical usage

molscope compare round4_integrated.csv round5_integrated.csv \
  -o round5_vs_round4 --reference-name round4 --candidate-name round5

What you get

A compare bundle with summary.json, summary.md, endpoint_deltas.csv, and a group-level delta table keyed by Scaffold_ID, Series_ID, or another grouping column. The current release-hardening pass also adds round_review.json and round_review.md so project meetings get explicit positive shifts, negative shifts, and low-coverage questions instead of only raw tables.

Control the comparison lens

molscope compare leads_reference.csv campaign_current.csv \
  -o current_vs_leads --group-column Scaffold_ID \
  --numeric-columns Measured_pIC50,Lead_LLE,CNS_MPO,Sol_ESOL_LogS

Use --numeric-columns to keep the summary focused on endpoints the team actively tracks, and --group-column to compare by scaffold, series, or cluster instead of only by whole-dataset means.

Where it fits

Compare sits naturally after assay integration and before reporting: molscope integrate gives you the unified campaign table, molscope compare identifies what changed, molscope learnings captures what the team should remember, and SAR or MMP workflows explain why the change is happening inside a scaffold family.
